## Something new I picked up

- AGENTS.md. The agent reads it on every request without being asked, so the rules go
  there once instead of into every prompt. Keep it to about five rules, and say what to
  do instead of what not to do.
- The Later list. When a client asks for something a static site cannot do, ordering,
  payments, logins, you agree to the goal, put the real feature under Later in the PRD,
  and ship a static stand-in now: a mailto link, a phone number. And the agent gets a
  rule for it too: if it needs a server, stop and tell me.
- Start a new chat when the job changes. A long interview followed by "now build it"
  in the same conversation drags in everything the interview ruled out.

## Something I noticed about working with AI

- Making the agent start every answer with my name. When it stops, the conversation
  has got too long and it has lost the thread, and that is the moment for a new chat.
  A cheap test I would not have thought of.
- Having the AI interview me instead of me prompting it flipped where the work is. The
  questions I could not answer were decisions I had not made yet, not gaps in the tool.

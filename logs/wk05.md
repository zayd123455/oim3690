## Something new I picked up

- GET vs POST. With GET the form data shows up in the URL, with POST it goes in
  the body. You can see the difference right away on the echo page.
- aspect-ratio plus object-fit: cover crops a photo in CSS without editing the
  image, and object-position picks which part stays in the frame. That fixed my
  hero photo. Also `required` on an input gives you validation with no JavaScript.

## JS I Don't Understand (from the To-Do App)

- `JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('todos')) || []` on line 6. I get that it's
  loading saved tasks, but not why it needs JSON.parse, or what the `|| []` does.
- `localStorage.setItem('todos', JSON.stringify(todos))`. Where is this actually
  stored, and why does it have to be turned into a string first?
- `(e) => { e.preventDefault(); ... }` in the submit listener. What is `e`, and
  what would happen without preventDefault?
- `todos = todos.filter((t) => t.id !== todo.id)` for delete. I don't fully get how
  filter removes one item, or what `!==` means vs `!=`.
- `id: Date.now()`. Why is the time being used as an id?
- The backticks with `${}` inside, like `` `${remaining} item${remaining === 1 ? '' : 's'} left` ``.
  The `? '' : 's'` part especially.
- `li.append(checkbox, text, deleteBtn)` and `list.appendChild(li)`. Why one uses
  append and the other appendChild.
- `list.innerHTML = ''` at the top of renderTodos. Why wipe the whole list and
  rebuild it every time instead of just adding the new one.
